Warner lists Lipatti’s fourteen-waltz collection with Barcarolle separately from his live Besançon recital. Cached EMI/Warner album0724356695658contains all fourteen waltzes in the same unusual LP order, then three extras; every selected track credits Lipatti and Chopin. Source notes identify the July1950Geneva studio family but attribute that date to external catalogue information, not an inspected original label. No exact session/take/mastering verification.
